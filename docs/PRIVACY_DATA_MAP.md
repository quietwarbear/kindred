# Kindred privacy data map

Last code review: 2026-07-31

This is an engineering inventory, not legal advice. “Production confirmation required” means the code supports or names the integration, but repository access alone cannot prove the active vendor account, contract, region, backup policy, retention setting, or console configuration.

## Verified deletion behavior and gaps

- `DELETE /api/auth/account` deletes the user's notification preferences, password resets, sessions, poll vote references, and user record.
- A sole community owner deletion also deletes events, announcements, chat rooms, subyards, kinships, memories, threads, payments, travel plans, budgets, invites, notification events, polls, and the community.
- A non-owner account deletion does not explicitly delete or anonymize every authored community record.
- The cascade does not explicitly delete all newer collections, including subscriptions, care circles, digest preferences/logical email-provider records, or vendor-side records. This requires engineering and legal review before promising complete deletion.
- Release 8 closes the Release 7 applicant-data gap: claimed `guest_family_claims` are removed; access requests replace the applicant key with a fresh random tombstone, remove name/relationship/operation data, and cancel a pending request without reversing an established community membership. Sole-owner community deletion removes both collections.
- Release 8 recap messages remain private family content after an individual author deletes their account, but author/update links become categorical tombstones. Next-gathering operation creator links are likewise removed. Sole-owner community deletion removes both Stage 8 collections.
- Release 9 removes deleted members' interest responses, clears and tombstones owned proposal content and identity, removes conversion actor links, preserves already-created drafts without proposer identity, and deletes all three Stage 9 collections on sole-owner deletion.
- SSO handoff codes are the only observed category with an automatic MongoDB TTL index.

1. Confirm MongoDB hosting, region, encryption-at-rest, backups, backup deletion, and log retention.
2. Confirm whether `GEMINI_MODEL` resolves to OpenAI, Google Gemini, or another LiteLLM provider in production, plus that provider's no-training and retention terms.
3. Confirm Google Analytics, GTM container tags, PostHog autocapture settings, property/project retention, IP handling, consent requirements, and regional transfer mechanism.
4. Confirm Stripe, RevenueCat, Apple, Google Play, Resend, Vercel, Railway, MongoDB, and push-provider DPAs and retention schedules.
5. Confirm whether children may participate through adult-managed community accounts and obtain counsel on COPPA and state minor-privacy obligations.
6. Confirm the lawful basis, notice/consent design, state “sale/share” analysis, and whether a cookie/analytics opt-out or consent manager is required.
7. Confirm statutory accounting/fraud retention and update the account-deletion cascade/wording accordingly.
8. Confirm the canonical branded support mailbox. Code centralizes the verified existing fallback `support@ubuntu-village.org`, but the domain does not match `heykindred.org`; no replacement address may be inferred.
