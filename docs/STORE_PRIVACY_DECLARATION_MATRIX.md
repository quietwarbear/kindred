# Kindred store privacy declaration matrix

Code and disclosure review: 2026-07-30
Authoritative engineering inventory: `docs/PRIVACY_DATA_MAP.md`

This matrix reconciles the shipped code paths with proposed Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data Safety answers. It is an engineering disclosure aid, not legal advice. “Confirm in production” means the repository names or initializes the processor, but source access cannot prove the live console configuration, contract, region, retention, consent, or processor-exemption status.

Kindred **does collect and process user data**. Any public or console statement that Kindred collects no data is inconsistent with the application.

## Identity linkage and purpose summary

- **Linked to identity:** account/profile information, community membership, events and RSVP records, user content, messages, purchases, push tokens, support communications, and PostHog product interaction after user identification.
- **Application functionality:** authentication, private communities, reunion planning, invitations, RSVP, itineraries, contributions, archives, notifications, purchases, and user-requested AI or SSO features.
- **Analytics:** GA/GTM and PostHog product interaction, plus diagnostics/performance only where enabled in production.
- **Communications:** transactional email, reminders, digests, delivery events, and support requests.
- **Account management:** registration, authentication, roles/membership, ownership transfer, subscriptions/entitlements, and deletion.
- **Temporary processing:** raw password before hashing; Google/Apple authentication assertions; payment credentials at the store/Stripe; short-lived SSO code; AI inputs while an invoked provider request runs. Derived identity, content, entitlement, or result records can still be stored.

## Retention and deletion statement for stores

Kindred stores most account, community, reunion, content, and subscription records without an automatic time-based purge. In-app account deletion removes the first-party records enumerated by the deletion endpoint. A community owner with other members must transfer ownership first. Sole-owner deletion cascades through the community collections currently handled by the endpoint. Non-owner deletion may leave authored shared content, and the endpoint does not explicitly cover every newer subscription or service record. Provider logs, backups, analytics, payments, email, AI processing, and legally required accounting/security records follow separate retention rules that require production and legal confirmation.

Do not promise complete removal within 30 days.

## Submission gates

Before either console is updated:

1. Inspect the live GTM container and PostHog project for tracking, autocapture, identity, IP, retention, consent, and regional-transfer behavior.
2. Confirm production Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Resend, Vercel, Railway, MongoDB, push, and AI-provider configurations and retention.
3. Apply Google Play’s current service-provider exemption only after the contractual and use restrictions are confirmed; otherwise answer “shared” conservatively.
4. Confirm Apple’s current definitions for tracking, Device ID, diagnostics, and developer communications.
5. Obtain legal approval for children/minor participation, lawful basis, consent/opt-out, sale/share, retention, and deletion wording.
6. Replace Google Play’s current “No data collected” declaration.
7. Keep all console changes unpublished until separately approved.
